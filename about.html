Behind the Code

System Architecture & Philosophy

Universal Load & Trim Calculation Engine

🛡️ Ironclad Privacy & The Tarmac Vault

Flight Training Organizations (FTOs) require strict data sovereignty. This tool operates on a Zero-Trust, Local-First Architecture.

  • No Accounts, No Logins: You will never be asked to create an account to run a calculation.
  • 100% Offline Capability: Using modern Service Worker caching, the entire calculation engine and FTO database are not vaulted.
  • Absolute Privacy: Your calculations and fleet data remain entirely on your device. We do not store, process, or transmit your load sheets to any external servers.

🏗️ Data-Driven Scalability

This app is not a hardcoded calculator; it is a universal math engine. By decoupling the UI from the data, it instantly adapts to any aircraft configuration—from a 4-seat Cessna 172 to a 7-seat Partenavia P68C—simply by reading a flattened JSON array.

Want your FTO's fleet added/edited? If you want your specific fleet added to the database, format your data using the JSON schema below and email it to feedback@evovhil.com.

🔒 Authenticity Check: To ensure strict data integrity, you must attach a recent photo or scan of your FTO's official Load & Trim sheet to your email. We will use this to verify the specific arms, limits, and stations before approving the pull request to the live database.

⚠️ WARNING — FOR EDUCATIONAL & TRAINING PURPOSES ONLY: While this calculation engine is built for high precision, it does not replace official documentation. The specific Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH) and the Pilot-In-Command (PIC) remain the absolute and final authority for all flight planning, weight limits, and center of gravity verification.

🚀 The Horizon

This v2.0 release stands as the definitive, offline-first calculation engine. However, the architecture is designed to scale. With enough user adoption and community support, the roadmap for this platform includes evolving into a full-fledged aviation learning ecosystem—complete with an AI-powered engine to dynamically generate sample exam papers for DGCA preparation.
